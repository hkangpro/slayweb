How it works

Four steps, and no way around them.

The order is the rule. Nothing reaches your books without the second step.

STEP 01

PROPOSE

Weave reads the feeds, classifies what is unclassified, and drafts the entry with the paperwork that goes with it.

STEP 02

APPROVE

It stops. A person opens the queue, looks, and approves or corrects. Nothing moves on its own.

STEP 03

EXECUTE

Only what was approved is written to the accounting, payroll and field systems.

STEP 04

LOG

Who approved what, and when. Kept in a form you can go back to.

Questions

Straight answers.

The four we get asked every time.

Do we have to change our ERP?

No. Weave does not replace your ledger. Acumatica or QuickBooks Online stays exactly where it is; only the repetitive work between systems comes to us.

Does the AI touch our books on its own?

No. Everything that reaches the books goes propose, person approves, execute, log. Matches the accounting system made automatically are checked again, and wrong ones are blocked.

Is this like Zapier?

No. Zapier moves things when a condition is met. The hard part here is not moving but deciding where it goes. Which jobsite and which line a receipt belongs to does not follow from a rule, and what is decided should not reach the books unchecked.

How do you split work with our accountant?

We are not an accounting firm. The monthly bookkeeping comes to us; judgement and filing stay with your accountant. Audit opinions and tax filings take a licence.
